Vary N (O(n) check):
The us/elem columns stay flat (~0.020) across 100 → 1 000 000 elements. That's your O(n) proof at fixed k

Vary k (O(log k) check):
time grows slowly as k doubles (99→200→350→390→530→671 for winner tree). Not perfectly log but the trend is there : O(log k) confirmed.


The loser tree wins at small N because adjust() stops early when the new element wins a match before reaching the root.
The winner tree catches up at large N because its update() always does a full root walk but touches memory
 sequentially — better cache behaviour once the data stops fitting in L1.
